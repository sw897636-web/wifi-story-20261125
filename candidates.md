# Candidates — Tell a Whole Story in 32 Characters

Contest: https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-tell-a-whole-story-in-32-characters-f86dd978
Judge criteria (in order): 1. ≤32 chars, reads as a joke that hints at a story;
2. joke lands on first read (clear twist/punchline); 3. tells a whole story
(situation + character + something that happened); 4. fresh (no stock Wi-Fi
jokes, or a familiar one turned new); 5. tight (every character earns its place).
Scores are /5 per criterion. Character counts verified with python.

## Round 1 — 8 candidates

| # | Candidate (chars) | C1 | C2 | C3 | C4 | C5 | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MyExStillPaysForThis (20) | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | **24** | Breakup happened; ex still pays the bill. "This" points at the network itself. Instant, petty, complete story. |
| 2 | ExTookTheDog_KeptTheWiFi (24) | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 22 | Split, dog gone, Wi-Fi kept. Good story, but needs a beat and the underscore costs tightness. |

| 6 | IHeardEverythingLastNight (25) | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 21 | Thin walls, neighbour overheard something. Good, but the event is vague — what happened? |

| 8 | LandlordRaisedRentAgain (23) | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 16 | A gripe, not a joke — no twist, no event beyond the obvious. |

Winner of round 1: **#1 MyExStillPaysForThis (24/25)** — best on the two
most important criteria (instant punchline, whole story) and the tightest.

Rejected on principle: stock Wi-Fi jokes (FBI van, "Pretty Fly for a Wi-Fi",
"It Burns When IP", virus/router puns) and shock-for-shock's-sake entries.
No digits reused from earlier bounties.

## Iteration 1 — wording variants of the winner

| Variant (chars) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| MyExStillPaysForThis (20) | **Keep.** "This" is self-referential: the name points at the very network the reader is looking at. "Still" carries the whole post-breakup timeline. |
| MyExStillPaysForThisWiFi (24) | Rejected. "WiFi" is redundant — the reader already knows it is a Wi-Fi name. Costs 4 characters, adds nothing (draft v1). |
| ExStillPaysForMyWiFi (20) | Rejected. Loses "My" ownership voice and the "This" self-reference; reads like a statement, not a confession. |

## Iteration 2 — stress-test the winner vs the runners-up

- vs #2 (ExTookTheDog_KeptTheWiFi): #2 has a slightly fuller story (the dog),
  but #1 lands faster (criterion 2 outranks 3) and is 4 characters tighter.
  The dog version also needs the reader to parse an underscore split.
- vs #3 (TheCatUnpluggedItAgain): #3 is charming but the "story" is one
  incident with no human stakes; #1 implies a relationship, a split and an
  ongoing financial indignity — a whole story, not a gag.
- vs #6 (IHeardEverythingLastNight): #6 hints at a juicier night, but the
  event is left too blank; #1 names the event (the ex kept paying) outright.

Final answer stands: **MyExStillPaysForThis — 20 characters.**
